How a run works

Two minutes. One Mega.

A rift is a copy of a map you know, with the colour pulled out and a rogue Mega somewhere inside. You get two minutes. The exit is the way you came in.

8:00

The rifts open

Three a morning, across seven spots in Tradewind, the National Park, the Ashlands and Shin-Tokyo. Lida rings when one opens. Step up to it and the game asks the only question that matters.

2:00

The world drains

Same map, no colour, no people. Wild Pokémon stand in the open and keep their colour. So does a shiny, gold against the gray. The clock starts the moment you arrive.

0:00

Claim it or lose it

The rogue Mega Mega Evolves on turn one. Knock it out for its stone, or catch it holding the stone. At zero the rift collapses and puts you back outside. Step in again and the same rift is waiting; only the shiny odds re-roll.

How to update.

Your save comes with you. The update patches your game in your browser and carries the save across; nothing in it touches a save slot.

1

Save in the game

Save from the START menu before you close it. Save states don't carry over to a new version.

2

Bring your game

Open Update my game and choose your Wishes of Tomorrow file from any version, or just your .sav. Nothing is uploaded.

3

Swap it in

The new version comes back under the same name, so your emulator keeps the pair together. Replace the old file with it. On Delta, add the new game, then import your save onto it.

4

Hit Continue

Not New Game. The rifts open at 8:00 every morning, in places you've already been.
