Reading the Pokédex

What counts as "can get"?

Anything you can catch, receive, snag or evolve inside the region, with the game's default settings. Randomizer Mode changes wild and trainer Pokémon, so this list only holds with it off. Evolutions that need an item only count when that item is sold or found somewhere in the region.

What do the percentages mean?

The chance that a wild encounter by that method, in that place, is that Pokémon. Fishing is split by rod.

How do Shadows work?

Shadow Pokémon belong to other trainers. Once you have the Snag Machine, any Poké Ball thrown at one snags it. The Shin-Tokyo patrols field a fresh team every time you fight them, and after the final boss their teams can include any Shadow in the Shadow Log.

Why does an act say "first available"?

It is the earliest act in which you can have that Pokémon. An evolution takes the act of the Pokémon it evolves from.
